Privacy & sign-in

How HatchHaven uses your Discord identity and what happens when you sign out.

Where sign-in happens

This GitHub Pages guide is public. Discord sign-in and sign-out take place on the HatchHaven account site hosted on Cloudflare. The session cookie belongs to that site.

What Discord shares

On the account site, the connect button opens Discord’s own consent screen. The site requests the identify permission to read your Discord user ID, username, and display name. Enter your Discord password only on Discord’s own sign-in page.

Your website session

Cloudflare stores those three identity fields in a short-lived website session. A random cookie connects your browser to the session. The login attempt expires in ten minutes. A successful session lasts up to eight hours. Signing out removes it immediately.

Discord access and refresh tokens stay out of browser storage and are discarded by the website after the identity lookup. The motion preference is saved only in this browser.

Your game progress

The bot keeps its own server-specific game saves. The website hosts the public catalogue. Signing in lets the site recognise your Discord identity while your inventory, trades, and Shellbits stay in Discord.

Disconnect your account

On the account site, select your account in the header and choose Sign out. To withdraw the Discord permission, open Discord’s User Settings → Authorized Apps, select the website’s app, and deauthorize it. Signing out there also clears an existing website session.
