ABOUT THE BOT

Welcome to the haven

HatchHaven is a Discord game about finding eggs, raising animals, and giving each new discovery a place on your plot.

YOUR FIRST HATCH

From egg to homestead

Type /start in a server that has HatchHaven. You’ll receive three Common eggs, ten incubators, and a small plot. Open /incubators, choose Start waiting eggs, then Collect ready hatches when their timers finish.

Your first animal could be a familiar farmyard friend. Higher ranks open up wild eggs with crystal stags, ember phoenixes, and stranger discoveries.

Life in the haven

Find wild eggs

Chat in a server channel with egg spawns enabled. When an egg appears, use its Steal button. Your collector rank determines which wild tiers you can claim.

Grow your plot

Place animals on owned tiles to earn Shellbits. Each copy has its own hourly rate. Use /plot collect to bank up to twelve hours of earnings.

Keep a field journal

Your collection remembers every species you discover, even after a trade or sale. Use /collection to revisit the journal and /trade to exchange animals with friends.

Your animals, plot, and Shellbits belong to the Discord server where you play. Discord sign-in identifies you on the HatchHaven account site. Your game inventory stays with the bot.
