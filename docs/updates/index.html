Potential updates

Ideas for future seasons, limited companions, and ways to earn them. These concepts are open for discussion.

SEASONAL CONCEPTS

Limited companion ideas

Lantern festival

Lantern-tail Tanuki

An anime-inspired night market. Complete lantern deliveries to earn festival stamps, then trade a full card for this paper-lantern companion.

Storybook season

Glasswing Fawn

A woodland fawn whose glass antlers catch the sunrise. Gather lost story pages through weekly quests to finish its illustrated chapter.

Midnight matinee

Starlight Projector Cat

A little cinema cat with a tail like a reel of film. Complete a set of movie-night challenges to redeem the companion.

Earn it or buy it directly

One proposed model gives every limited pet a visible quest target and a guaranteed redemption. Players who prefer a shortcut could buy that same named pet at a fixed price. Both versions would have the same appearance and stats.

The current game uses fictional Shellbits. These proposals have no checkout, release date, or active paid rewards.

Guest collections

Anime, Disney stories, Pokémon, and movie worlds are possible themes for a licensed guest season. A crossover would need an agreement with the rights holder before characters or artwork could appear.

The three concepts above are original HatchHaven ideas. They have no announced partners.
