Getting started

/startStart a collection in this server and receive three Common eggs. Open /incubators to hatch them.
/start

You begin with ten incubators and a 5 × 5 plot. Running /start again opens your existing profile.

/incubatorsStart waiting eggs, check their timers and collect the creatures that are ready to hatch.
/incubators

Choose Egg storage to start a particular egg. Timers keep running offline. Finished eggs occupy their slots until collected. Last hatch results reopens your latest batch.

/helpOpen a private overview of egg tiers, hatch times and the first steps for building your plot.
/help
/guideOpen the private in-game command guide. Choose a category and a command to see its arguments and how to use it.
/guide

Animals & collection

/inventoryBrowse the animals in storage. Inspect individual copies, compare their earnings, mark favourites, place animals or review a sale.
/inventory [search]
search Optional
Part of a stored animal’s name. Leave blank to view all stored animals.

Use the menu to sort by name, value or income, filter by rarity or favourites, and sell several copies at once. Sell duplicates keeps one of each species.

Try /inventory search:Rabbit

/collectionOpen your permanent discovery journal. Find silhouettes of undiscovered species and inspect the lore and stats of animals you have found.
/collection [search]
search Optional
A rarity or part of a discovered species name. Leave blank to browse the full journal.

A discovery stays in the journal after you sell or trade away the animal. You can review hatch counts, plot income ranges and base hatch odds here.

Try /collection search:Uncommon

/sellChoose one owned animal, review its Shellbit sale price and confirm the sale.
/sell <creature_name>
creature_name Required
Select the copy to sell from the suggestions. Each lists its income and copy label or plot coordinates.

An animal reserved for an active trade must be released from that offer first. Selling keeps the species in your discovery journal.

Try /sell creature_name:Chicken

Plot & income

/plot viewOpen your plot and minimap. Move the 5 × 5 view, check earnings or select a visible animal to return to storage.
/plot view

Each axis runs from 0 to 29. Your starting land spans 12 through 16 on both axes.

/placePlace an animal from storage on an empty tile you own so it starts earning Shellbits.
/place <creature_name> <x> <y>
creature_name Required
An animal in your inventory. Choose a particular copy from the suggestions if you own several.
x Required
Horizontal tile coordinate, a whole number from 0 to 29.
y Required
Vertical tile coordinate, a whole number from 0 to 29.

This is the shortcut for /plot place. An animal held in an active trade must be released first.

Try /place creature_name:Chicken x:12 y:12

/plot placeMove a stored animal onto an empty, unlocked tile. Its individual hourly income starts counting towards your plot earnings.
/plot place <creature_name> <x> <y>
creature_name Required
An animal in your inventory. Choose a particular copy from the suggestions if you own several.
x Required
Horizontal tile coordinate, a whole number from 0 to 29.
y Required
Vertical tile coordinate, a whole number from 0 to 29.

Each tile holds one animal. To move one that is already placed, use /plot remove first.

Try /plot place creature_name:Rabbit x:13 y:12

/plot removeReturn a placed animal to your inventory. Its copy keeps its hourly rate for the next time you place it.
/plot remove <creature_name>
creature_name Required
Choose an animal on your plot. Suggestions show the plot coordinates and hourly rate of each copy.

Try /plot remove creature_name:Rabbit

/plot expandReview the Shellbit price and confirm the purchase of a locked tile that shares an edge with your land.
/plot expand <x> <y>
x Required
Horizontal tile coordinate, a whole number from 0 to 29.
y Required
Vertical tile coordinate, a whole number from 0 to 29.

Your first expansion costs 200 SB. Each tile you buy adds 25 SB to the price of the next one.

Try /plot expand x:17 y:14

/plot collectMove the Shellbits earned by your placed animals into your wallet. The bot sends the amount to you privately.
/plot collect

Plot income continues while you are away, with up to 12 hours credited for an uninterrupted absence.

Rewards & upgrades

/rankReview the next rank and its price. Confirm an upgrade with Shellbits to claim the next wild egg tier and unlock five more incubators.
/rank

Ranks are purchased in order. Eternal Ascendant opens Eternal egg claims and brings your capacity to 50 incubators.

/questOpen your private board of three daily and three weekly quests. Refresh your progress, then use each completed quest’s Claim button.
/quest

Rewards include Shellbits and eggs. Claim them before the daily reset at 00:00 UTC or the weekly reset on Monday at 00:00 UTC.

/dailyClaim 1,000 Shellbits and one Common egg. The next claim becomes available 24 hours later.
/daily

You need one free egg storage slot. The reward egg appears in /incubators.

/weeklyClaim 5,000 Shellbits and one Uncommon egg. The next claim becomes available seven days later.
/weekly

You need one free egg storage slot. The reward egg appears in /incubators.

/monthlyClaim 20,000 Shellbits and one Rare egg. The next claim becomes available 30 days later.
/monthly

You need one free egg storage slot. The reward egg appears in /incubators.

/shopBrowse 50 luck and incubator items, buy them with Shellbits and activate them from My item bag. Open Cosmetics to buy or equip a profile design.
/shop

Buying a consumable adds it to your bag. Luck affects new wild egg claims, speed affects eggs you start, and timer items reduce the time left on running incubators.

/profile viewDisplay a collector card showing progress and equipped cosmetics for you or another player in this server.
/profile view [player]
player Optional
Choose a server member who has started playing. Leave blank for your own card.

Try /profile view player:@Friend

/profile customizeSet a rank title, feature a discovered species or use its portrait as your avatar. With no options, this opens the avatar picker.
/profile customize [title] [creature_name] [avatar]
title Optional
An unlocked rank title. Leave blank to keep your current title.
creature_name Optional
A species from your permanent journal to feature on your card. Leave blank to keep your current selection.
avatar Optional
A species from your permanent journal to use as your portrait. Leave blank to keep your current avatar.

The picker also lets you switch back to your Discord avatar.

Try /profile customize avatar:Rabbit

Trading

/tradeInvite another player in this server to trade creatures and Shellbits. Omit the player to reopen your active trade.
/trade [player]
player Optional
Choose another human member who has used /start in this server. Leave blank to resume an invitation or trade.

Each player can join one trade at a time. Offers allow up to 25 unfavourited animals from storage and expire after ten minutes. Both players must confirm again after either offer changes.

Try /trade player:@Friend

Casino

/casino rulesRead the rules, odds and payouts for coinflip, slots and blackjack in a private message.
/casino rules

New games are available only in channels where the server has enabled them. Stakes use fictional Shellbits. There is a five-second cooldown between new games.

/casino coinflipChoose Heads or Tails and place a Shellbit bet in an enabled channel. A correct call returns 1.9 times your stake.
/casino coinflip <bet> <choice>
bet Required
A whole-number stake from 10 to 10,000 fictional Shellbits, in multiples of 10.
choice Required
Choose Heads or Tails. Both are equally likely.

Payouts include the stake. An incorrect call returns zero.

Try /casino coinflip bet:100 choice:Heads

/casino slotsSpin three reels in an enabled channel. Three matching symbols return 18 times the stake, and exactly one pair returns 1.45 times the stake.
/casino slots <bet>
bet Required
A whole-number stake from 10 to 10,000 fictional Shellbits, in multiples of 10.

Each reel has seven equally likely symbols. No match returns zero. Returns include the stake and round down to whole Shellbits.

Try /casino slots bet:100

/casino blackjackStart a blackjack hand in an enabled channel. Hit, stand or double down on your first two cards.
/casino blackjack <bet>
bet Required
A whole-number stake from 10 to 10,000 fictional Shellbits, in multiples of 10.

The dealer stands on all 17s. A natural pays 3:2, a regular win pays 1:1, and a tie returns the stake. Your hand stands automatically after three minutes.

Try /casino blackjack bet:100

/casino resumeReopen your most recent blackjack hand. Continue a live hand or review the outcome if it has already ended.
/casino resume

An expired hand settles as a stand. A saved hand can still be finished if new bets have been disabled in that channel.
